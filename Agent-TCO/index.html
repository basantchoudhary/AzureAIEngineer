Reading the bill

At this volume, the model isn't the biggest cost

At 2,000 conversations a day, fixed platform costs (Search, hosting, endpoints) are more than half the Azure bill. Set conversations to 20,000 and watch the model take over: fixed costs barely move.

Output and reasoning tokens dominate the model cost

There are about 13× fewer of them than input tokens, yet they cost more. Lower reasoning_effort, ask for short answers, and cap max_output_tokens before trimming input.

The semantic ranker is easy to forget

It's billed per query after 1,000 free a month. Every turn that searches is a query, so at this volume it costs almost as much as the Search service.

People usually cost the most

A fifth of an engineer for monitoring, prompt fixes and incidents is close to the whole Azure bill. Total cost of ownership includes them.
